Welcome to TechVault

TechVault: Showcasing ECE Students' Prototypes and Innovation is a digital platform created to showcase and organize innovative prototypes developed by Electronics Engineering students.

TechVault provides a centralized space where students' projects can be documented and easily explored according to their respective courses and categories.

What is TechVault for?

TechVault is designed to preserve and present the creativity, skills, and innovations of ECE students. It allows users to explore information about different prototypes, including their descriptions, purposes, components, members, schematic diagrams, and prototype pictures.

How to Use TechVault

Use the Search Prototype bar below to quickly find a prototype. You may also use the Categories section to browse projects according to their course.

Teachers or authorized users may use Add Prototype to register a new student project.

About TechVault

TechVault: Showcasing ECE Students' Prototypes and Innovation is a digital platform created to showcase, organize, and document innovative prototypes developed by Electronics Engineering students.

The platform provides a centralized space where ECE student projects can be presented according to their respective courses. Users can explore project descriptions, purposes, components, members, schematic diagrams, and prototype pictures.

About Our Group

We are a group of Electronics Engineering students who developed TechVault to promote creativity, innovation, collaboration, and technological development among ECE students.

Through this platform, we aim to make student prototypes easier to organize, access, and explore.
